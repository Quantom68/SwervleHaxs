About Swervle

Swervle is a daily racing game where precision driving meets chaotic physics. Chase the perfect run on the daily track and cross the finish line the fastest way you can.

You can play right in your browser. No account needed to give it a go. Learn the corners, try a different line, and see how much time you can take off your run.

The daily race

The map resets at midnight Central time. Everyone gets the same track, and you can make as many attempts as you want. Your best time is the one that counts on the leaderboard.

The tracks are procedurally generated, a bit like Minecraft. A seed creates the map for that day, including its terrain and biome. Some days you're racing through woods, other days through a desert or the highlands. You have to learn what the new track gives you.

Keep chasing your time

Pick another racer on the in-game leaderboard to race their ghost. See where they brake, how they take a corner, or where you can find a faster line. The ghost gives you something to chase without getting in your way.

Old tracks stay available. The day-of board records the race as it stood at midnight. The all-time board includes later attempts, so you can come back and improve. The results pages have the boards, finish-time stats, and available race videos.

Make it yours

Sign in to keep your name and results across devices. Open the paint garage to paint your vehicle and save it to your account. You'll see other players' cars and names out on the track and on the boards.

The daily race is free. Vehicle packs and monthly support are optional. Swervle is for players aged 13 and over.

Come say hi

We share updates, runs, and things we're working on in Discord. Tell us what you like, what broke, or show us a run you're proud of. You can also find streams and videos on the community page.

If you need a hand, start with How to play or the FAQ. For game or account help, visit Support or email help@swervle.com.
