FAQ

When does the map reset?

Midnight Central time. The old track stays available if you want another go.

How do I sign up?

Open the account menu in the game and choose Sign in. Use Google or email to create your account.

How do I link my Discord?

Join our Discord and use /link. Follow the link from the bot and sign in to your Swervle account.

How are the maps created?

Procedural generation, like Minecraft. A seed builds the track, so everyone gets the same map for that day.

How do I make the game run better?

Open Menu > Settings > Graphics and try Medium or Low. Lower quality changes the visuals, not the physics.

On a computer, turn on graphics acceleration in your browser and restart it. In Chrome, go to Settings > System. In Edge, go to Settings > System and performance. Enable "Use graphics acceleration when available."

In Firefox, find Performance in Settings. Uncheck "Use recommended performance settings" if needed to show the hardware acceleration option, then enable it and restart.

What's the difference between day of and all time?

Day of counts runs made while that track was the daily. It locks at midnight Central. All time includes later attempts, so you can keep chasing a better time. Medals belong to the day-of board.

How do I paint my vehicle?

Open your car menu and choose PAINT/SELECT VEHICLE. Make it yours, then save it to your account.

How do I race another player's ghost?

Pick a racer on the in-game leaderboard to load their ghost. Follow their line, find a faster one, or press G to hide ghosts.

How do I get my Twitch to show on the leaderboard when I'm live?

Add your Twitch handle to your game profile, stream in the Swervle category, and race. Give it a minute to show up.
